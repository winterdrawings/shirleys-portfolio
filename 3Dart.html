3D Art | Fall 2025 and Fall 2026

Here is where I have made these artwork in a 3D space. One of the peices is made using blender and the other peice is using unscented soap bar. The soap bars are made from art 13 class with Andrew Marcovich and blender is made in Art 102 with David Bayus. For the soap project, we had to make a normal, abstract and detailed bar soap. I decided to go with a seashell (the middle) for abstact and a camera (on the right) for the detailed soap Each if these seperate pieces has it's own challenges but in the end I enjoyed making the person or item in 3D space. My favorite 3D medium to use is Blender since I can make items in the 3D space and use my 2D character reference sheets to create the Character in 3D form.
