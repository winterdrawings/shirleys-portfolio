Artist Bio

Shirley Yee is a student at San Jose Sate University majoring in Digital Media Art with a minor in Photography. She was born in San Francisco, CA and grew up in Alameda, CA. During that time period, she found her love and passion for art. She loved making crafts with being uninentionally messy. While she was in middle school, she has been drawing simple sketches on her drawing book or on a random paper that is lying around. Her passion for drawing and creating art continues during her sophomore year of high school; when one of her assignments was to create an illustration drawing on Adobe Illustrator. She realized that there are other applications for her to expand her creativity on Ibis Paint, she wanted to create more. She decided to explore the digital art feild to expand her skill set for a future career in art.

During her free time, she spends time with her friends but also make art for her art friends. She also takes photos with a DSLR camera on the side to help her friends create long lasting memories and to freshen up with what she has already learned in the past.
