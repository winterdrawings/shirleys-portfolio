Artist Statement

I have loved making any type of art since I was a child. From traditional to digital, it was a way for me to escape from the stress that I have to deal with in real-life responsibilities. Art has also helped me with just thinking creatively and making something that has never been made before. Seeing other artists work growing up has been an inspiration for me to create art for commissions or for the people I love. It brings me joy when people have received, look, then smile and let me know that they have really enjoyed my art. My goal is to make art for the gaming industry and help then create characters or to use CSS to make art for the company even if CSS isn't my strong skillset. I want my artwork to be in a game where people can enjoy seeing the creation of my art in design and in a 3D space. I enjoy making and editing clips into a video and working in a 3D space. Seeing my 2D character illustration come to life and have it printed makes me so happy. I also draw character emotes for Twitch streamers who would like one. I am in the process of working on an art commission. This client requested a starting screen for her Twitch channel. My other goal is to be a freelance artist no matter what type of practice it is. Being a freelance artist and having clients while making a stable income is my dream career to have.
